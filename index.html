HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  SEARCH AND AI VISIBILITY

What Google and AI answer engines can and cannot read on Deconstruct today

Checked on the home page, the shop all page and three product pages. Each point has a public link so your SEO team can confirm it in a minute.

  • Reviews never show as stars in search. Product schema on the Vitamin C Serum, Clearing Serum and Oil Control Face Wash carries price and stock but no aggregateRating or review. 1,327, 734 and 497 reviews sit on those pages and none of it is machine readable, so no star snippets and nothing for AI answers to cite. Rich Results Test Schema validator
  • The brand entity reads as thedeconstruct. Every Product schema names the brand thedeconstruct, and the same string is used as image alt text on the home page. Search and AI see a different entity from the Deconstruct people actually search for. Schema validator
  • Part of the Vitamin C copy uses look-alike letters. The long-form copy inside the Vitamin C PDP accordions has 66 Cyrillic e characters across 46 words, including serum, routine, sensitive and consistency. They look identical but index as different words, so that copy ranks for nothing. Open the PDP Unicode checker
  • 113 collections are indexed for 67 products. Holi, Republic Day, Valentine's, Mother's Day, Ugadi, Christmas, Rakhi and a dozen flat-25 and flat-35 sale collections are all in the sitemap. They compete with the real category pages and spread crawl budget and link equity thin. Collections sitemap
  • Thin and empty pages are live and indexed. /collections/trial-kit has zero products, /pages/our-result renders 10 characters, the sunscreen fan challenge page renders its heading only and also exists at a misspelled URL, and a Shopify theme demo page is published on the brand domain. trial-kit our-result demo page
  • The home page H1 is empty and the share image is the favicon. The home page carries an H1 tag with no text in it. Home and shop all both use the favicon as og:image, served over http, so every WhatsApp, social and AI link preview shows a tiny logo. OG preview, home
  • The Vitamin C and Clearing Serum FAQs have no FAQ markup. Both pages carry ten real questions and answers inside accordions, with no FAQPage schema. The face wash page already has it, so the pattern exists on the theme and just is not applied everywhere. Schema validator
  • The AI-facing files are defaults or broken. llms.txt returns a 503 error, and AGENTS.md is Shopify's generic text with no products, bestsellers or policies. Our automated browser was also served a Cloudflare challenge on the home page, which is worth checking against the AI crawler allow list. llms.txt AGENTS.md

Every ad click lands on a generic PDP, and the old campaign pages never got taken down

Roughly 200 live creatives on Meta, and every visible destination is thedeconstruct.in, blinkit.com, instagram.com or amazon.in. Big Skincare Fest, Sale Ka CEO, Monsoon Mega Sale, the Rakhi hamper, Buy 2 at 599, up to 35% off combos and a Malayalam sunscreen push are all running at once, and none of them has a page of its own.

The site carries 113 live collections against 67 products. Navratri falls on 11 October and Diwali on 8 November. There is no page for either.

HIGHEST PRIORITY  ·  LIVE ON SITE

Three campaign pages are live, indexed and effectively empty

/pages/deconstruct-sunscreen-fan-challenge renders 35 characters, which is its own heading and nothing else. /pages/our-result renders 10. /collections/trial-kit returns a live page with zero products in it, which is the page a first-time buyer would look for by name. All three sit in the sitemap.

LIVE ON SITE

A discontinued guarantee is still being sold on the site

/pages/money-back-guarantee still runs a full sales page for a no irritation promise and a 100% refund including shipping, with two SHOP NOW buttons, under its own banner saying the policy ended on 25 April 2025. It has been live in that state for 17 months and it is still linked and indexed.

COMPOUNDING

Dead festive pages accumulate while the next festive moment has none

holi-offer, republic-day-sale, valentines-special, women-day-sale, mothers-day-sale, ugadi-sale, summer-carnival-sale, back-to-campus, independence-day-sale, birthday-sale, face-serum-christmas-sale and monsoon-sale-1 are all still live, alongside three overlapping big-skincare-fest collections and about a dozen flat-25, flat-30, flat-35 and upto-50 discount collections. The Rakhi ads were still running on 22 September. Raksha Bandhan was 28 August. City pages exist for Delhi, Chennai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad, so the instinct to target by city is already there, while the ads run in Malayalam, Kannada, Tamil and Telugu with no page to match.

PROBLEM 03  ·  CONVERSION BARRIERS
Solved by TrooCRO →

Where the Vitamin C Serum PDP can convert more of the traffic it already buys

Written from inside the buyer's head: oily and acne-prone, around twenty, half of them men, spending ₹262 to ₹599. Each of the eleven points is one thing she looks for and what giving it to her does, strongest three first.

ROUTINE FIT

Tell her it works with the niacinamide she already owns

Deconstruct's face wash, Clearing Serum and Fluid Sunscreen all carry niacinamide, and can I mix it with Vitamin C is the question she arrives with. A small works-with grid by the button answers it. She adds the serum to her routine instead of leaving to Google.

PROOF

Show results on a face like hers, and like his

The results photo is one woman, and the ads are half men dealing with acne marks and tan. Add results from two or three Indian skin tones, including a man. Buyers trust proof faster when they see their own face in it.

BREAKOUTS

Say won't clog pores where acne-prone buyers look

The serum is proven non-comedogenic, but that lives as a certificate on gallery slide 16. Put it in the first fold beside the skin type. The acne-prone buyer's biggest fear is answered before she scrolls.

See the remaining barriers found on this PDP
WEEK ONE

Tell her what is normal in week one

At pH 3 to 4 a mild tingle is normal early on. One line saying a tingle is fine and burning means stop keeps beginners using it instead of quitting or leaving a 1-star.

FRESHNESS

Sell freshness, the one thing a marketplace can't

Vitamin C is best used fresh. Direct from us, freshest batch, with the manufacturing month, gives her a real reason to buy here instead of from marketplace stock.

CREATORS

Bring the Reel creators onto the page

She clicked a creator saying it isn't greasy on oily skin. Two or three of those clips near the top pick up the conversation she was already in.

SKIN PHOTOS

Ask for week-four skin photos, not bottle shots

Most review photos show the bottle. Rewarding week-four skin photos with Dcoins and pinning the best turns reviews from I bought it into it worked.

PER DAY

Turn ₹599 into a per-day number

Price-sensitive buyers think per day. Saying how many weeks a 30ml lasts at 2 to 3 drops makes ₹599 read like a small daily cost.

INTENT

Lead with oily skin, the reason she clicked

The ads sell a Vitamin C that won't feel greasy. Say it in the first fold, water-light and made for oily skin, and ad visitors get their answer in one glance.

PRICING

Make the 10ml an easy first order

Show price and per ml on each size, and offer a face wash add-on to take the ₹262 10ml past ₹300. She gets free delivery, you get a bigger first cart.

CLARITY

State the discount once

One 25% off applied badge by the price. She stops looking for a missing code and moves to checkout.
